## REPLACED (1) — original killed, rival asserted in report

### R1. Heren XVII fleet instructions "included" Portuguese-damage clauses + "secret orders to the Cape"

**Killed original:**
> "The Heren XVII designed new instructies (instructions) for outgoing fleets
> during their meetings, including clauses about inflicting damage on the
> Portuguese and other objectives. Secret orders were also sent to the Cape for
> incoming fleets."
> (stated source: https://vocwarfare.net/thesis/3/communication)

**Why killed (unanimous kill across all four lenses):** The source states the
**exact opposite**. At the autumn 1661 meeting the directors, preparing for peace
with Portugal, designed new instructions for the outgoing fleets that **struck
out** all the clauses about inflicting as much damage as possible on the
Portuguese. The "secret orders sent to the Cape for incoming fleets" element does
not appear in the source at all; the covert traffic described ran to **Batavia**
(via the *Nieuwenhove*) and to **Ceylon**, and the directors' secret resolutions
survive only for the late 18th century.

**Asserted in its place (report Section 4, HIGH):** The 1661 autumn meeting
designed new fleet instructions that **removed** the Portuguese-damage clauses as
peace preparation, while off the record commissioning war fleets to Ceylon (1661)
and Mozambique (1662). No Cape element.
Source: https://vocwarfare.net/thesis/3/communication

Reviewer re-verification (read directly): "in the latter meeting all kinds of
preparations for the peace were already decided upon, such as designing new
instructions for the outgoing fleets, striking all the clauses about inflicting
as much damage as possible on the Portuguese."

  (parent: Claim 7). Struck as a mis-citation: that URL contains no mention of
