## Methodology / unit / referent  (the high-stakes lens here)
| referent | the "~1,200" figure | KEEP as CAPTIVES, not deaths | Coen's exact words: "In alles hebben ontrent 1200 zielen van haer becomen; daerenboven sijn noch verscheyde andere doot gebleven." "becomen" = obtained/captured ALIVE; the dead are counted SEPARATELY ("besides these, various others were killed"). Confirmed by the 16 Nov letter: "van Slamma gelicht 1200 sielen" (lifted from Slamma) vs "Ontrent 2500 ... vergaen" (perished). A provenance-only pass that reads "1200 souls" as a death toll would be WRONG — the referent lens fixes it: 1,200 = Bandanese taken captive/deported (chiefly the Slamma population), shipped via the Dragon to Jacatra/Batavia. |
| unit | Riebeeck "oppercoopman ... voor opperhooft" | KEEP | rank = oppercoopman (chief merchant); function on the voyage = "voor opperhooft" (as commander/chief). Both are in the heading; report states both, not one as a substitute for the other. |
