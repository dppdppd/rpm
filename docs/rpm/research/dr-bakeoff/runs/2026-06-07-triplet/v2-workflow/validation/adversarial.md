## Lens-tally summary

- Claims where all four lenses voted **keep**: C-24, C-26 (2 of 30).
- Claims with at least one **kill** vote: C-2, C-3, C-5, C-6, C-8, C-15, C-16, C-17, C-18, C-19, C-20, C-30 (12 of 30).
- Most common kill driver: **wrong-source / referent-not-in-named-source** (the claim's fact was often real but pinned to a page or document that did not contain it). This affected C-5, C-6, C-16, C-17, C-18, C-19, C-30 especially.
- Second driver: **meeting-function or title misattribution** (autumn vs spring in C-15; Governor vs superintendent in C-30; instructie vs appointment-deed in C-27).
- The three Dutch-primary sub-question cores (C-1 seat allocation, the Banda hill figures + 1,200 referent under C-6/Banda page, and the Riebeeck heading under C-8/C-9/C-12) all survived to assertion once re-anchored on the correct primary artifact (Dutch original octrooi; NA Banda hertaling; DBNL Bosman & Thom heading).
