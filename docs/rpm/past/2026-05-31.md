## Key Discoveries
- **Over-kill in deep-research verification tracks SOURCE TIER, not voter correlation.**
  Measured across two domains (voc-decline tertiary-sourced history; draftyard
  primary-sourced software-UX), 14 claims, zero clean over-kill canary. With tertiary
  sources provenance over-kills (a Principle-3-correct refusal); with primary sources
  over-kill is 0/6 everywhere. Perspective-diversity does not reduce it.
- **Perspective-diversity's real payoff is recall on present-but-wrong claims:** on draftyard
  the methodology + alternative-hypothesis lenses each caught a referent-stretch AND a ranking
  contradiction that provenance/consistency/recency missed (panel 2/2 vs weakest single lens
  1/2) — a #3-metric gain, not #4's claimed over-kill one.
- The committed OR-kill panel rule is optimal on good sources, inert on weak (provenance
  dominates the kill set). Majority-voting cuts over-kill only by collapsing orphan recall
  4→1. The real over-kill lever is the provenance absent-vs-tertiary split.
- A structural "validation" that grades a gate with the gate's own logic is tautological;
  the blind replay (an LLM following the prose, grader held out) is the real behavioral proof.
